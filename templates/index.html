ABOUT THE CLINIC

Compassionate medical care with a personal touch.

Zenova Health Care Centre & Clinic provides trusted medical support for chronic condition management and long-term wellness, with home visits and care planning for patients who need support at home.

Led by Dr Rahul Anand R M and Dr Radhika E, the clinic focuses on practical treatment, preventive guidance, and patient-centered follow-up care for individuals and families.

Expert-led care

Guidance from experienced medical professionals in chronic and preventive care.

OUR SERVICES

Focused solutions for better health outcomes

From chronic disease management to diagnostics and home-based support, we deliver integrated care that is practical, timely, and patient-centered.

Hypertension management

Personalized monitoring and treatment plans for blood pressure control and long-term cardiovascular health.

Diabetes management

Complete diabetes care including lifestyle counseling, medication support, and ongoing follow-up.

Diabetes wound care

Focused care for diabetic foot and wound management to promote healing and reduce complications.

Thyroid treatment

Assessment and treatment plans for thyroid imbalance with regular review and monitoring.

Weight management

Structured guidance for sustainable weight reduction and healthy lifestyle improvement.

Lab diagnostics

Accurate diagnostic support and test-based care planning for timely clinical decisions.

Home visits

Arrange an in-home clinical visit when travelling to the clinic is difficult. Contact us to discuss your needs and visit availability.

Home care

Care planning and follow-up guidance for ongoing health needs, chronic conditions, or recovery—coordinated with the clinic team.

Hairfall treatment

Assessment and care guidance for hair loss and scalp concerns.

Skin care

Personalized assessment and treatment guidance for common skin concerns.
